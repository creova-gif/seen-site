SEEN by CREOVA

You're seen in the app.

SEEN is a home for community stories in English, French and Spanish. Read and listen to stories, follow the people who tell them, explore collections, and find funding for storytellers.

Where stories live, where culture breathes.

What is SEEN?

Stories in three languages

Read or listen to story worlds in English, French and Spanish, and switch the app's language at any time.

Creators and collections

Follow creators, save stories, and explore collections gathered around a theme.

Funding for storytellers

Browse Canadian funding programs for storytellers, each linked to the funder's own page, and keep track of your applications.

For creators

Tell your story on your terms.

Write and publish stories with image descriptions and content notes, so more people can take part. Your credit and your consent come first.

Before the beta opens, we are working on:

  • Permissions and credit for everyone who worked on a story
  • A way to challenge a credit
  • Privacy settings you control
  • Reporting, and removal of content that should come down

For viewers

Open a story and start reading or listening.

SEEN works on small phones and is built with access in mind.

  • Interface in English, French and Spanish
  • A larger text option
  • Playback speed controls
  • Tools to report something that isn't right
